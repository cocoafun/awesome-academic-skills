# Academic Writing & Research Workflows

Workflows and tools that help users turn research inputs into structured outputs such as outlines, reviews, and drafts.

- [K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-writer](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-writer) — Deep-research writing workflow for generating publication-ready papers, reports, posters, proposals, and literature reviews. `Writing` `Workflow`
- [Future-House/paper-qa](https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa) — Scientific-literature QA workflow that produces cited answers useful for evidence-constrained drafting, note-taking, and review support. `Writing` `QA` `Citations`
- [K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-skills](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-skills) — Reusable scientific skills for research and writing tasks. `Writing` `Skills` `Claude`
- [Orchestra-Research/AI-research-SKILLs](https://github.com/Orchestra-Research/AI-research-SKILLs) — Research skill library that also supports repeatable reading-to-writing workflows. `Writing` `Skills` `Research`
