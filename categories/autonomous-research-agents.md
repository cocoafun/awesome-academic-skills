# Autonomous Research Agents

Projects that move beyond reading support into more autonomous research loops such as ideation, coding, experimentation, reporting, and critique.

- [HKUDS/AI-Researcher](https://github.com/HKUDS/AI-Researcher) — Autonomous scientific research system spanning literature review, idea generation, implementation, validation, result analysis, and manuscript preparation. `Agent` `AI Scientist` `Workflow`
- [LearningCircuit/local-deep-research](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research) — Deep-research agent for multi-source investigation across academic search, web sources, and local document collections with self-hosted deployment options. `Agent` `Deep Research` `Local`
- [SakanaAI/AI-Scientist](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist) — Autonomous research-agent project for idea generation, experiment execution, paper drafting, and self-review. `Agent` `AI Scientist` `Experiments` `Writing`
- [SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v2](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v2) — Follow-on AI Scientist system focused on workshop-level automated scientific discovery via agentic tree search. `Agent` `AI Scientist` `Search`
- [assafelovic/gpt-researcher](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher) — Autonomous web-research system for gathering sources and producing detailed reports, useful for related-work surveys and background synthesis. `Agent` `Deep Research` `Survey`
- [stanford-oval/storm](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm) — Knowledge-curation system that researches a topic through multi-perspective questioning and produces citation-backed long-form reports. `Agent` `Survey` `Citations`
